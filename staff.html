FOUNDER // PHILOSOPHY · CODE · DESIGN · GAMES

Jin

▸ PORTFOLIO ▸ X · @JINZUREI

Jin is the founder of Setun Labs, and the lab is his answer to a question he has been circling for years: what would AI look like if it were built to be trusted instead of merely believed? His bet, stated plainly: the next leap comes from structure, not scale. Models small enough to run on hardware you already own, constrained so tightly they cannot say what they cannot prove.

He has been building toward this in public for years. Ignis, his fully local AI assistant, runs its own inference and carries episodic and semantic memory. That is proof he already ships AI on ordinary hardware. I Am The Fool, a game engine he wrote from nothing with custom frame-rate-independent physics, is proof he owns every layer of every system he touches. His sites carry GLSL shaders he wrote himself, his client work shipped end to end from Figma prototype to deployment, and this site, every pixel and every line of it, is his.

He thinks like a philosopher and ships like an engineer. Before a system is built he asks what it claims about the world. Before an interface is drawn he asks what it means for a user to know something through it. His working rule is that the unexamined code is not worth shipping, and he means it literally: the lab pre-registers its tests, kills weak designs early, and refuses to publish anything it cannot measure.

That is the person behind the promise. The ternary architectures, the verification stack, the app, all of it carries one name, and a public record of everything he has built stands behind it. He is not asking you to trust a brand. He is asking you to read the work.
