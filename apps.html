// OPEN-CORE ◈ STATED PLAINLY

Everything you run is open, FSL-1.1, converting to Apache-2.0 two years after each release. Here is exactly where the line falls:

Open · the app The entire mobile app (Android; iOS in progress): chat, models and recommender, downloads, benchmarks, pairing, device dashboard, agents, settings, themes, tutorial, RAG, vision, memory, the phone-control stack. Every screen and every animation is in the repo. ◈ FSL-1.1
Open · the desktop The DESK console (all nine decks), hubctl (every command), and hubd, the daemon itself, the heart of the system. ◈ FSL-1.1
Open · the protocol Every wire frame is documented, pairing, chat relay, tools, decisions, tasks, audit, sync, device metrics, phone generation, phone control, the pool. Anyone can build a client against it. ◈ DOCUMENTED
Open · data & seams The device-metrics API is stable, documented, read-only. The benchmark JSON schema, the CascadeJudge interface, the tool-pack format, the CLI registry, the catalog file, all open and user-editable. ◈ YOURS
Closed · two things Deliberately: the calibration tuning behind CascadeJudge (the interface is open; the tuning is the trade secret), and the future hosted benchmark-aggregation service (nothing in the app depends on it; no open feature is gated on it). ◈ THE BUSINESS
The rule If a closed piece vanished tomorrow, the open product loses nothing. ◈ HELD TO
